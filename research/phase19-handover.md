# Phase 19 — ten-item handover

17 October2026. Nicholas Ham programme; internal Codex research, not external
peer review or author sign-off. Foundation and companion repositories unchanged.

## 1. General acquisition/incentive attempt

P19-I reduces the entire permitted uniform capability mechanism class to two
obedience moments and a scalar log-partition certificate. It is a solved finite
symmetric benchmark, not a universal agent-information theorem. Trusted
acquisition, private addressed delivery and state-ignorant workers are explicit.
P19-E extends the mechanism equivalence to hidden erasure self-information.

## 2. Complete reliability frontier

[Full proof](phase19-information-frontier.md): with tau=(c+delta)/B and
sigma=(c-delta)/B, exact success is feasible precisely on
[max(0,(1-N*sigma)/(1-sigma)),1]. Optimal mean work is
min(s/tau,N-(1-s)/sigma), with sigma0 treated separately. Two adjacent
cardinalities and one common posterior-odds tilt attain the exact minimum.
Exact and at-least targets can differ; both-binding preferred reliability is
identified. Algebraic optimal probabilities are allowed mathematically and
not replaced by rounded rational tight-IC tables.

## 3. Private/public information value

[Heterogeneous two-worker proof](phase19-asymmetric-information.md) gives exact
full-success minima for arbitrary full-support prior and worker thresholds.
Hiding list size saves strictly iff tau1+tau2>1 and both thresholds are below1.
The additive saving is at most one bit; the public/private information ratio
is unbounded even with fixed positive obedience margins. Broader private
persuasion advantages are prior; this exact small criterion is an extension.
Acquisition cost excludes constructing supplied communication channels.

## 4. Nondegenerate equilibrium-payoff separation

None obtained. [P19-G](phase19-payoff-geometry.md) instead proves a compact
closure for complement-invariant own-flip games: CE payoff region equals raw
payoff region clipped by ui>=1/2. Directed-cycle coordination has genuinely
nonconstant CE payoffs and a linear lift. This stops the parity/coordination
hardness lead. Observer encoding and unproved compact externality baselines
were rejected. Ordinary CE, binding max-margin CE and recommendation marginals
remain distinct. No approximate-EF separation is inferred from exact bounds.

## 5. Efficient small protected projections

[P19-P](phase19-projection-optimization.md) optimizes any linear objective on
P18's disjoint-role projection by evaluating s=0,c+delta,1, then uses the prior
threshold/matching construction to recover at most2k+2 profiles. O(k) rational
arithmetic for target optimization, polynomial recovery/bit length. This is a
direct corollary; no universal small-dimensional projection claim.

## 6. Exploration beyond geometry/frontier polishing

[P19-E](phase19-exploration.md) proves the hidden self-test architecture exactly
rescales reliability: C_hidden,alpha(s)=C0((s-alpha)/(1-alpha)). Certain-success
acquisition is unchanged for every alpha<1, then drops at alpha1. If a common
availability flag is coordinator-observed, independent of capability, certain-
success acquisition instead becomes (1-alpha)C0(1). Knowing WHEN others know
can be valuable even when the availability flag itself contains no capability
information. Free exogenous self-tests/monitoring are specified resources;
no endogenous truthful reports or decentralized implementation were proved.

## 7. Exact checks and independent attacks

Twenty-four new exact tests (244 total): seven frontier, nine self-test, four
geometry, three asymmetric and one projection-optimization. Reciprocal internal
proof reconstruction passed; concrete boundary checks and source access limits
are in [AG-P19-REVIEW](../reviews/AG-P19-REVIEW.md). Rational witnesses, formal
prime-log expressions and integer-cleared conjugate inequalities supplement
analytic proofs. An algebraic-root bracket tests implementation limitations.
No external validation or novelty clearance is implied.

## 8. Targeted novelty assessment

[Assessment](phase19-assessment.md) classifies P19-I as the strongest provisional
task-specific completion, P19-A as a technically distinct small extension,
P19-E as a scoped architecture equivalence, and P19-G/P19-P as elementary
corollaries. Primary versions actually inspected are recorded in branch notes;
standard information design, list entropy, KL conjugacy and symmetry averaging
receive credit. Exact task priority remains unresolved. No new matching or
approximate extension-complexity theorem.

## 9. Updated broad map

Information, incentives and constrained sensing first; nonuniform N>2/noisy
private signals/verifiable reporting next. Representation second, with natural
ordinary-CE payoff separation still open. Decentralization/learning need their
own implementation guarantees. Symmetry and fairness remain canonical in their
read-only companions; information opportunity does not automatically imply
welfare or utility fairness. No LLM behavior or empirical performance claim.

## 10. Strongest direction and publication strategy

Prioritize endogenous information, but obtain a substantial generalization and
closer task-assignment priority audit before proposing an ambitious paper. Keep
the representation outline as a separate modest possibility. Do not begin a new
manuscript or enlarge the existing11-page foundation to collect results. No
external contact, authorship change or submission performed.
