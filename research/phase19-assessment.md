## Actual source comparisons and access limits

The frontier note records actual sections inspected in Armenter, Müller-Itten
and Stangebye's author-hosted 2021 working version of *Geometric Methods for
Finite Rational Inattention* (including task assignment), Bunte–Lapidoth's
list-coding work and the prior Sason comparison. It does not substitute an
unread final version for the working text. These establish the relevant
standard framework/entropy machinery, not priority of the present closed form.

The exploration note compares actual own-type-conditioned BCE obedience in
Bergemann–Morris 2016 and the privately informed receiver model in the
Kolotilin–Mylovanov–Zapechelnyuk–Li 2016 primary manuscript. Own private
information and information design are prior territory. Hidden independent
availability with a coordinator unable to condition acquisition on it is the
specific architecture here; no general information-ordering novelty is claimed.

The geometry note records the actually inspected Lehrer–Solan–Viossat 2011
corrected proofs, Proposition1; arbitrary CE payoff-polytopes are prior, but
vertex enumeration does not preserve the requested compact-input/compact-
baseline contract. It also retains the inspected Kamisetty–Xing–Langmead 2011
joint-moment formulation from P18. Exact matching/stable-set lower-bound
sources remain those inspected in phases17/18: no new lower-bound comparison
or approximation theorem was obtained this phase. P19-P adds no new matching
principle. The asymmetric note's *Pivotal persuasion* comparison is limited to
the primary abstract/displayed description, explicitly not full reconstruction.
Source links/version details reside alongside each proof. Absence from these
comparisons provides NO evidence of originality.

## Broad mathematical map

Information/incentives is now first: complete uniform frontier, heterogeneous
concealment criterion and private self-test architecture are connected by an
operational acquisition problem. Nonuniform N>2, endogenous verifiable reports,
noisy self-information and constrained sensing remain genuine gaps. Preserve
acquisition, private delivery, public disclosure and existing knowledge as
separate resources. A trusted private coordinator is supplied, not implemented.

Representation remains second: exact recommendation-marginal gaps are valid
with their constant-payoff/weak-dominance caveats. Ordinary CE in genuinely
nonconstant PAYOFF coordinates is unresolved. Binding-max-margin examples do
not solve it; neither observers nor unproved externality baselines qualify.
Approximate EF needs its own robust theorem; exact Rothvoss cannot be relabelled.
Small retained projections can collapse; one natural class now has an exact
linear-time optimization formula, rather than an assertion that low dimension
is always easy.

Decentralization and learning remain open implementation questions, not
consequences of private obedience. Symmetry is an optimization tool here and
canonical group classification stays in Game Symmetry. Opportunity/fairness
questions about unequal information availability belong in the research map,
but no welfare/utility comparison or canonical Game Fairness theorem follows.
Persistent-type incentive restrictions remain scoped; the old discounted
memory example remains known prior and stopped. No empirical LLM prediction.

## Ranking and decision

Prioritize the endogenous-information programme. Its strongest current result
is a complete solved finite symmetric benchmark, not yet a general theory of
agency. A prospective information paper would need substantive nonuniform or
private-information generalization plus a sharper task-assignment literature
comparison. P19-E is a useful architecture warning and extension, not sufficient
alone for an ambitious paper. P19-A is supporting detail.

Keep the modest representation paper as a separate possibility, preserving
its existing outline only. Stop the parity/coordination hardness route under
P19-G. Do not begin another manuscript, rename the foundation or expand it to
list these results. The original11-page foundation and both companions remain
unchanged. No submission, external contact, author change or novelty sign-off.
