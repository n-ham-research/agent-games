## Cavaliere: primary working body obtained, final version still unchecked

The [SIEP February2000 working paper](https://www.siepweb.it/siep/wp/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/1395874217004.pdf) is26 pages, SHA256
`90a4c086f9c3314ceed6491fca43a73515b2c9c1b7237e795a8b2d9861ffa036`.
Root inspected §2 and visually checked PDF pp8–11 (printed7–10); the
continuation reviewer independently checked these passages. Its threshold
contribution model uses common cost and benefit; Proposition1 restricts attention
to symmetric CE. It discusses efficient CE/pure-NE lotteries and then
spillovers. This is direct public-good-correlation prior, not a verified statement
of the heterogeneous protected-payoff hypergraph theorem. The2001 published
[article](https://doi.org/10.1111/1097-3923.00065) full body was not retrieved;
the working paper is not represented as identical to it.

Do not import every preprint claim. For M=2,w=1, Proposition1's displayed
count-only formula forces cost1/2, whereas the symmetric one-worker lottery
obeys at costs1/4 and3/4. Likewise a failure pure NE at threshold2 makes
unrestricted pure-NE-mixture efficiency false; restrict the lottery's support.
Exact checks preserve these source cautions. They concern this working text,
not an assertion about corrections or claims in the unseen published article.

## Other closest mathematics and theorem-level decisions

* **Protected regions:** Kerman–Tenev2024's persuasion support reduction is
  already recorded as close prior for unprotected thinning. Ostroy–Song2006
  supplies generic worst-CE-payoff LP duality. P14-A's candidate extra content
  is preservation of arbitrary designated payoff vectors plus reliability,
  yielding a bounded-column description and an FPT(k) algorithm from an
  explicit minimal-winning hypergraph. Generic CE polytopes and linear
  programming are not new. No equivalence to the full payoff region is claimed.
* **Cost blindness:** endpoint obedience is standard robust-incentive reasoning;
  P14-B's candidate increment is an attained all-type payoff envelope in a
  specified coalition class, and the counterexample separating hidden signal
  pooling from public-cell/fully blind analysis. Taneva–Wiseman and robust-CE
  literature are inspected in the continuation note. No new universal theorem
  about information being harmful or helpful is claimed.
* **Ordinary-accuracy query bounds:** Hart–Nisan2013 is direct prior for
  deterministic/randomized equilibrium query separation. P14-Q's candidate
  restriction is player transitivity, actual action-orbit count2 and constant
  approximate invariance. It is an oracle lower bound allowing unlimited
  computation, not explicit-circuit constant-accuracy computational hardness.
  Zero-error randomization is distinguished from high-confidence Monte Carlo.
* **Interchangeability:** probabilistic bisimulation/zigzag maps, strategy-
  restricted Nash preservation, perfect-recall mixed/behavioral realization and
  finite-horizon coupling bounds are established. P14-I is a scoped finite
  operational synthesis with explicit private observations, deviations and
  resource limits; it is not promoted as a new general bisimulation theorem.
  Branch source versions, theorem locations and original-Kuhn access limits
  are recorded in phase14-interchangeability.md.

## A current primary comparison discovered during the wider search

Rudov–Sandomirskiy–Yariv, [Extreme Equilibria: The Benefits of Correlation](https://www.lyariv.com/papers/ExtremeEquilibria.pdf), 58-page author manuscript accessed16October2026.
Root inspected the payoff-polytope/generic Pareto-improvement discussion and §6's
symmetric binary construction. It studies when Nash can be improved through
correlation and the geometry of CE payoffs. It is important current prior;
it does not directly supply the protected-deletion representation in the
passages inspected. Generic regularity and symmetric/count-utility restrictions
must not be silently substituted for arbitrary monotone tasks with heterogeneous
costs. This was a targeted comparison, not a line-by-line review of all58 pages.

## Source and publication decision

The largest Phase13 source gap is now narrowed by an actual primary Cavaliere
working body, with final-version uncertainty retained. Reading more sources
cannot establish novelty by absence. The protected-region representation is the
strongest positive candidate to compare next; the oracle restriction is a
secondary theoretical candidate. The contextual model is a foundation for a
new resource-preserving question, not evidence that the mature bisimulation
literature has been surpassed. Keep the manuscript conservative and unchanged.
