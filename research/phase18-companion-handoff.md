# Conditional handoff — assignment resources, symmetry and information

16 October2026. Read-only Agent Games note for the canonical Game Fairness and
Game Symmetry programmes. No companion file or theorem status is changed.

The sequential exploration studies independently specified PATHWISE discounted
workload balance, not equal utilities or a definition of fairness. At rational
discount above1/2 its exact autonomous finite-memory obstruction substantially
overlaps known target-discounted-sum results; see phase18-sequential.md for the
actual2026/2013 source chain. It is useful for specifying assignment timing and
resource assumptions, not a new foundational fairness theorem. Ex-ante role
impartiality is much cheaper and is not logically equivalent to pathwise balance
or equal long-run frequencies. Heterogeneous costs break utility equality even
when work burdens are identical; A1/U1/U5 calibration caveats remain essential.

The capability model in phase18-endogenous-information.md gives a symmetric
optimal acquired experiment with equal ex-ante worker marginals, yet strictly
positive information acquisition can remain necessary. Statistical role
symmetry does not reveal which worker is capable. The criterion is full task
success plus obedience and an information budget; no moral fairness claim or
new interchangeability equivalence is inferred from that symmetry.

A worthwhile companion-owned question would specify an agent's common
consequence/assignment preference and determine which workload criterion it
actually justifies. Agent Games supplies the resource counterexamples and
precise models, not a new canonical answer. No external communication or
companion modification was performed.
