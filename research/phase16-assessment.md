Homogeneous positive-margin hardness is a meaningful stronger restriction than
P15-S4: it removes cost heterogeneity and weak-obedience dependence. Optimizing
its numerical constant alone is not the point. Its proof remains elementary
once classical dummy recognition is known. Generic optimum-CE hardness is not
new, and a failure of a sufficient tractability criterion is not a hardness proof.

## Publication decision

The programme now justifies planning a focused contribution-game paper, but
NOT opening an additional manuscript or expanding the foundation draft yet.
The working title suggested by the review remains a suggestion. A plausible
core is protected-payoff structure, a concrete deviation-preserving tractable
class and natural homogeneous robust hardness. The critical missing gate is
an exact novelty assessment showing a sufficiently substantial increment over
compact-CE, read-once coalitional and public-good literature. No inspected
source has been shown to state the entire package; absence of a matching search
hit is not evidence of originality. The standalone hardness transfer is too
modest to support the desired ambition without more structural mathematics.

Disclosure is a viable competing direction. Seek a theorem beyond embedding
a balanced partition into a fixed-law two-volunteer incentive constraint.
Resource substitution should pursue approximate reconstruction with budgeted
deviation transport or a useful synthesis class; another exact observer/coding
reformulation would repeat established theory. No new repository or manuscript
was created. No researcher contacted, author changed or paper submitted.

## Concrete stopping and next-work rules

1. Complete an exact P14-A/individual-worst-CE comparison, including published
   public-good sources when accessible. Do not stall new mathematics on one
   unavailable final PDF or treat a working version as the final.
2. Investigate read-once extensions with controlled repeated-worker interaction:
   a repeated variable invalidates the simple sensitivity lift; a constructed
   bounded-width deviation encoding, not raw task width, is needed.
3. Test disclosure with two independent tight incentive rows: rank-two privacy
   already covers the scalar transportation theorem. Charge the decoder's
   guessing objective and distinguish exact safety from approximation.
4. Do not escalate the resource branch into a paper until it beats an existing
   observer/synthesis/coding theorem in a meaningful model or guarantee.
