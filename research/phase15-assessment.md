# Phase 15 — contribution ranking, broad map and publication decision

16 October 2026. Mathematical proofs, internal review, source comparisons and
practical implementation are separate evidence. Novelty remains provisional.
The existing eleven-page manuscript is unchanged.

## Ranking after investigation

1. **Protected continuation geometry with a natural representation boundary.**
   P14-A remains the strongest positive structural candidate. P15's threshold
   flow representation and coverage-universe formulation extend computation
   beyond listed minimal winners, even to the full utility region. Their core
   algorithmic method is established compact-CE/DP machinery. The strongest
   fresh negative candidate is NP-hard constant-additive approximation of ONE
   worker's minimum CE payoff for binary-weight thresholds, with fixed bounded
   costs. It provides a consequential limit to k-only succinct generalization.
2. **Information-constrained credible coordination.** Exact private-channel
   implementability and safe public releases give a coherent model. Private
   information can be ignored; compulsory public disclosure changes obedience.
   Strictly safe disclosures can fail jointly. These are concrete results with
   a robust-margin analysis, but Blackwell/BCE/information-design prior is
   substantial; not yet an independently novelty-cleared theory.
3. **P13-Q/P14-Q computational restrictions.** Retain circuit fine-precision and
   oracle constant-accuracy zero-error results as separate statements. New
   succinct contribution-game payoff hardness is a different optimization
   problem and does not unify them into a stronger invariant-NE hardness theorem.
4. **Resource-aware substitution.** Constructive adapter budgets and sharp cut
   storage/communication bounds repair an actual limitation of perfect-recall
   equivalence. Exact factorization algebra is specifically prior. Useful
   modelling progress, not presently evidence for a new general bisimulation
   or finite-state synthesis theorem.

No ranking entry is external verification, priority clearance or author sign-off.

## Symmetry and fairness: precise useful connection

Read-only canonical baselines remain Game Fairness
f75ec9f7f0ff3b74226baed67a2727eccf0220d3 (U1/U5, A1, P7-A) and Game Symmetry
e896a5cae79c5c63a81be16f1577bc365e67a5a6. Neither repository changed.

For a supplied cardinal game automorphism group preserving a chosen protected
set J setwise, CE (and a common conditional-margin delta) is convex and invariant.
Averaging any law therefore gives an invariant law with utility vector equal to
the group's average of the original vector. In particular, an invariant feasible
payoff target remains feasible with an invariant law. A group-invariant concave
objective such as the minimum of calibrated protected utilities weakly improves
under averaging. Proof: each transported law satisfies the transported linear
obedience inequalities; average the rows, then apply concavity. This is AG-S1/
AG-O2 established averaging, not a new theorem. For noninvariant specified
individual targets averaging changes the target; transitivity alone does not
preserve it. Sampling the averaged law still requires correlation and a means
of privately delivering actions; splitting supplies neither by itself.

A common externally justified payoff floor, calibrated shortfall, or opportunity-
related linear constraint can be added to the flow LP, and max-min floors by an
auxiliary variable. This is an algorithmic consequence conditional on choosing
those requirements, not a definition of fairness. Positive affine changes
preserve CE but alter unnormalized cross-player sums/equality: U1/U5 and A1's
calibration caveat therefore remain essential. Equivalent local opportunity
sets do not by themselves supply global game automorphisms (P7-A). No new
canonical fairness characterization is claimed by Agent Games.

## Applications with required architecture

A delegation coordinator that knows the task and costs can compute a private
recommendation law for target reliability and calibrated payoff limits in the
tractable classes. Distributed verification may use a small coverage universe
of required checks. Shared-resource coordination may use a threshold capacity
with unary weights. These applications require commitment to the law, private
recommendation delivery, common cardinal utility model and unilateral obedience;
they are not decentralized protocols or empirical agent studies. Type-dependent
plans, elicitation, collusion and communication constraints need separate models.

Resource substitution offers a checkable finite adapter contract when tool and
permission interfaces are explicit. It controls controller modes and deviations
within the stated budgets; it does not bound CPU from mode count or treat an
external helper's storage as free.

## Paper recommendation

Keep the foundation manuscript conservative. The strongest prospective focused
paper is on credible contribution-game payoff regions: protected normal forms,
explicit-hypergraph FPT, natural succinct formulations and the binary-weight
constant-gap boundary, with information constraints if they support the same
central question. Before drafting, complete the closest worst-CE/public-good
novelty comparison. The tractability method alone is insufficient originality.

Keep resource substitution as a separate research programme for now. A separate
paper needs a substantial theorem beyond known transducer composition,
nonnegative rank and elementary storage coding. The oracle symmetry result may
need a separate computational narrative; do not force it into a payoff-region
paper or declare a paper split merely because branches differ. No submission,
external contact, authorship change or new manuscript is authorized or performed.
