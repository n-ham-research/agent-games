those coordinates form a cube. CE and even CCE force support on independent

face; Rothvoss gives2^{Omega(sqrt N)} exact LP extension complexity. Both sides
use exactly the SAME own-action marginals and physically allow collisions.
This genuinely differs from P17-X's already-hard nonstrategic region.

Caveats are central: activation is weakly dominated and all equilibrium payoffs

The complementary moment-lift lemma is also recorded: a compact nonstrategic

correlations. This is a simple general limitation, not a new universal theorem.

## 2. Fixed-small payoff projections

phase18-projections.md proves an exact constructive collapse on the SAME K_(2m)
coverage family whose FULL payoff/success region was hard. Protect k disjoint
edge-workers with m>=k+2, common cost c and0<=delta<=min(c,1-c). Its region is

    0<=s<=1,    max(s-c,delta*s/(c+delta))<=u_i<=s for each protected i.

O(k) inequalities suffice; every point has a polynomially constructed law on
at most2k+2 profiles using threshold coupling and protected matching completion.
This includes one/two protected payoffs WITH reliability. No minimal-cover list,
LP solver or inherited lower bound is assumed. Incident roles/smaller residual
graphs are outside the proved construction; an explicit failed law is retained.

One payoff WITHOUT reliability is always an interval with two inequalities,
although its endpoints may be NP-hard to compute. This demonstrates why
existential EF size and efficient coefficient construction/optimization differ.
No fixed-two-coordinate superpolynomial EF family was proved this phase.

## 4. Endogenous information and incentive design

phase18-endogenous-information.md studies N uniform possible capable workers,
exactly one capable, nobody initially knowing even their own capability. The
mediator JOINTLY chooses acquired experiment and private work law; success
requires the capable worker's action, common benefit B/cost c, conditional
margin delta. State cannot be ignored when selective work must be obedient.
Without incentives everybody can work for certain success at zero acquisition.

For certain success let r=min(N,B/(c+delta)), k=floor r,lambda=r-k. The sharp
minimum acquired mutual information is

    log N-[(1-lambda)log k+lambda log(k+1)],

with the endpoint r=N handled directly. If worker-list cardinality is public,
the minimum rises to log N-log floor r. Private pooling of adjacent list sizes
attains the former. A second regime gives exact low-success slope
I_min(s)=(s/tau)D_Ber(tau||1/N) for0<=s<=tau under the explicit additional
conditions in the note. Experiments verify MI symbolically via prime logs.

Variable-list Fano and KL tools are established; the strategic cap/attaining
laws and exact scoped curves are candidates, not a new information inequality.
Capability agnosticism, ideal signal acquisition and private addressing are
substantive resources. This is not truthful cost elicitation or a physical
sensor design. Nonuniform/private-self-awareness/general-reliability regimes
are not solved.
