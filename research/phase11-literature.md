# Phase 11 — primary-source comparison and access limits

16 October 2026. These comparisons narrow novelty claims; none is a clearance.

* Tewolde, Zhang, Oesterheld, Sandholm and Conitzer (AAAI 2025), [long author manuscript](https://emanueltewolde.com/files/symmetries.pdf), §5 and Appendix E, Theorem 9, PDF pp8,23–24: action-orbit polynomial systems already give invariant approximate Nash algorithms. AG-O1 adapts the method to explicitly tabulated additive local factors, reducing degree to interaction arity. AG-O1b's q-only FPT reinterpretation also applies to their explicit normal-form input after deleting singleton-action players: do not advertise prior work as intrinsically XP-only. AG-O1c optimizes welfare only inside the invariant Nash set. Exact real-algebraic sampling is an external black box, not implemented here.
* Brandt, Fischer and Holzer, [Equilibria of Graphical Games with Symmetries](https://webspace.maths.qmul.ac.uk/felix.fischer/publications/bfh_graphsymm.pdf), author preprint 27 January 2010, Definition 3 and Theorem 6: local neighborhood-count symmetry has a polynomial mixed-equilibrium algorithm in its stated action regime. This is a different promise from arbitrary supplied typed automorphisms of additive factors; neither class generally contains the other. Originality of the factor adaptation remains unresolved.
* Papadimitriou–Roughgarden, [author paper](https://theory.stanford.edu/~tim/papers/sym.pdf), §§3–4, and Jiang–Leyton-Brown, [arXiv:1109.6064v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.6064), §§2–3: compact correlated-equilibrium computation and deviation-adjusted welfare are established. AG-O2 is a transparent orbit LP, not a replacement for their algorithms; orbit enumeration can itself be exponential.
* Battaglini–Palfrey, Organizing for Collective Action: Olson Revisited, JPE 132(9), 2024, DOI10.1086/729580; [26 July author manuscript](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~trp/Olson_27July2024%20wiith%20appendix.pdf), §2.2 printed pp9–11. Joint reporting/action deviations are already part of honest-and-obedient mechanisms. Footnote12 explicitly averages mechanisms preserving surplus. Flat-success/flat-workload is an immediate specialization of their interim IC constraints. AG-M2/M5 adapt this reasoning to finite protocols and heterogeneous independent costs with a general upward-closed success family; do not claim a new screening principle. The current note labels these P11-M2/M5.
* Bergemann–Morris (2016), Theorem1: information-expansion/Bayes-correlated-equilibrium results do not turn an externally informed mediator into a truthful private-report mechanism.
* Beyhaghi–Cai, [Pandora's Problem with Nonobligatory Inspection: Optimal Structure and a PTAS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.01524), v1, 3 December 2022, introduction and Theorem1.1 inspected by root. Selecting an uninspected alternative is already an established search model. AG-V1 is its iid binary calibration after transforming costs to prizes, with an exact expected-query frontier; no general optimal-search novelty is claimed. Their general distributions, structural theorem and approximation scheme are substantially broader.
* Basu, [2011 real-algebraic survey](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~sbasu/raag_survey2011_final.pdf), Theorem2.15: singly exponential sample-point arithmetic bound. The coefficient-bit/exact-output sampling specification is invoked in its standard form, also used by Tewolde Appendix E. Full original Renegar publisher text returned403, Cornell TR858 returned405, and the original BPR author-book link failed. This is a source-verification gap to close before publication, not evidence that a solver has been implemented or independently benchmarked.

The full notes preserve assumptions, proof details and actual inspected locations.
AG-H1 is an explicit standard-style counting reduction, not an originality
claim about succinct-game hardness. AG-S1 and the finite-seed divisibility
criterion are established averaging/sampling facts. No literature search can
establish novelty by absence; citations are selected comparisons, not exhaustive.
