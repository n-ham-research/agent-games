## Rejected or unsupported claims retained

- No fixed mixed NE under all exact game automorphisms: false in finite games;
  established Nash theorem removes this unrestricted obstruction.
- Potential existence or maximization implies welfare efficiency: refuted by
  preserved feasibility public-good model.
- Common positive unanimity-payoff control has only two pure NE for all n≥3:
  false at n4 due0011; AG-E5 now restricted to n3.
- Nonsplitting intrinsically forces positive coordination cost: false with
  state feedback/inverse actuators; split extension can still incur cost.
- Optimal task allocation always needs less information than symmetry testing:
  false; row-space/rank comparisons and both directions are explicit.

Open conjectures are not manuscript theorems. Primary next questions concern
costed decentralized private information, verification/incentives and partially
observed persistent-state feedback. See open-questions.md.
