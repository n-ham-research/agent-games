## Protected regions and succinct computation

The closest task-specific thinning source remains Kerman–Tenev2024, Lemma1 and
Appendix C, inspected in Phase14. It restricts persuasion recommendations to
minimal winning coalitions in its voting model. P14-A instead preserves an
arbitrary designated payoff/action projection while thinning other workers in
a contribution-game CE. This is a precise difference of statements, not proof
that no earlier source contains the latter. Cavaliere2000 common-cost threshold
CE and generic CE-payoff polytope literature remain relevant.

Primary compact-CE comparison advanced materially this phase:

* Papadimitriou–Roughgarden, [January2008 author version](https://timroughgarden.org/papers/cor.pdf), abstract/introduction: compact CE computation and optimal CE for anonymous/symmetric and bounded-treewidth subclasses are prior. Finding some CE and optimizing a specified payoff are distinct.
* Jiang–Leyton-Brown, [WINE2011 extended abstract](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~kevinlb/papers/2011-WINE-OptimalCorrelated.pdf), §3 Proposition1 and §4.1: deviation-adjusted welfare separation yields optimal CE. Our total-weight and coverage-count path constructions make the relevant coefficient optimization additive, hence are established-framework applications. An explicit primal flow formulation and task parameter can be useful without constituting a new LP principle.
* Matsui–Matsui, [ORSJ2000 primary article](https://orsj.org/wp-content/or-archives50/pdf/e_mag/Vol.43_01_071.pdf), §4 Theorem2 and §5: weighted-voting non-dummy recognition hardness and pseudopolynomial weight DP are classical. Our parity/weight1 relevance gadget is not an originality claim. The candidate increment is the singleton-backed constant-gap reduction to one worker's MINIMUM CE payoff, with bounded fixed costs; it needs a separate worst-equilibrium-payoff comparison.
* Tanaka–Matsui, [arXiv2101.11180 primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.11180), abstract/formulation: pseudopolynomial network LP for weighted-voting least core is close methodological prior. Cooperative least-core allocations are not contribution-game correlated equilibria. A different solution concept does not make flow encoding itself novel.

* Barman–Ligett, [arXiv1504.06314v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.06314), §4/§4.1 inspected by the succinct branch: general approximate-CE quality hardness and terminal-aggregate-conditioned DP are particularly close prior. Their positive class requires bounded aggregates and bounded utility influence; binary threshold jumps need not meet both after normalization. Our positive flow formulations are adaptations, while S4's candidate specificity is the natural threshold class and one-agent constant gap.

Inspected passages do not by themselves state our protected-thinning theorem
or constant-gap payoff reduction. That bounded observation is the current
comparison; no exhaustive negative literature claim is made. The positive
coverage/threshold algorithms should be presented as adaptations accompanying
a task-specific structural or hardness result, rather than headline algorithmic
novelty absent further evidence.

## Information and substitution

The information note compares its channel factorization and private-information
monotonicity with Blackwell and Bayes-correlated-equilibrium prior. Safe-public-
release constraints are conditional obedience rows, not a newly invented general
information order. The strict noncomposition witness and posterior-sensitive
robustness application are concrete candidates; their simple ingredients and
adjacent information-design/privacy literature need acknowledgement.

The resource note located exact close prior: Cohen–Rothblum1993 Theorem3.2 is
already the stochastic-factorization/nonnegative-rank equivalence, including
normalization. Therefore P15-R2's algebra is PRIOR, not a new theorem. Finite
transducer composition, reverse deviation simulation and elementary one-cut
storage bounds are also established ingredients. The scoped resource contract
is useful programme development but does not yet justify a separate original
paper. Rational versus real factors, allowed external memory and repeated-read
permissions are material restrictions, not implementation details to hide.
