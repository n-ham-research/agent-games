## Ranked contribution prospects after the new investigations

1. **Representation-dependent positive/negative boundary.** P17-T1 constructs a
   single-exponential FPT full-region LP in the NUMBER of repeated workers in
   a monotone formula. P17-X gives an unconditional exact-LP size lower bound
   for read-twice formulas, contrasting P16's read-once linear formulation.
   P17-T2 additionally separates a single all-deviation terminal summary from
   local factorized representations. These are precise different contracts,
   not one invented parameter asserted to characterize all strategic complexity.
   The strongest new candidate statement is the read-twice payoff-region face
   bound. Its lower-bound engine is exactly Rothvoss, and face transfer is
   elementary; task-specific priority is still unresolved.
2. **Protected payoff structure with the existing natural hardness boundary.**
   Useful supporting mathematics, downgraded in expected originality rather
   than presented as a fresh general purification/complexity breakthrough.
3. **Safe disclosure and joint-design architecture.** Multi-row randomized
   guessing has a direct polynomial LP; two independent volunteer constraints
   cannot be replaced by one scalar aggregate. Direct-decision merging is
   established persuasion methodology. Redesigning a payoff-irrelevant law
   can remove the old fixed-law obstruction completely. Useful boundaries,
   not a new general information-design algorithm.
4. **Decentralized selection as a genuine competing question.** Honest local
   computation of a strict terminal equilibrium does not implement equilibrium
   of the message game. A private-cost, always-success/exactly-one obstruction
   and an election-deviation witness expose that gap. These use established
   mechanism/cheap-talk principles; no learning or repeated-equilibrium
   convergence theorem was found.
5. **Resource substitution.** Active but deliberately stopped from producing
   another default-error/TV/observer corollary. No genuinely new approximation
   synthesis guarantee emerged this phase. Existing exact records are retained.

## What the structural results do and do not explain

For any efficient binary task evaluator, all pure switching gains take only
n+1 task evaluations. The hard step is not necessarily calculating one
profile's incentive data. It can be representing the convex hull exactly,
optimizing an objective under equilibrium, or implementing communication.

The read-twice lower-bound face already exists WITHOUT incentive constraints.
Thus we have NOT proved that incentives intrinsically increase extension
complexity relative to the feasible task-outcome polytope. We have proved
that a small formula/easy success evaluation cannot ensure a small full CE
payoff LP, even with every variable repeated only twice and identical costs.
Polynomial optimization and exact extension complexity are different: matching
itself is the established counterexample. The old voting-based CE boundary
also has easy physically optimal welfare/backstop equilibrium, sharpening a
different distinction between task optimization and individual CE objectives.
Neither observation is a universal theorem that strategic complexity is greater
than task complexity in every model.

## Updated broad research map

| Theme | Phase17 evidence or stopping decision | Next consequential question |
|---|---|---|
| Coordination/incentives | Honest read-once distributed optimum versus private-cost ex-post implementation obstruction | Permit quantified failure/redundancy or repeated future tasks and seek a resource-charged equilibrium protocol. |
| Information/communication | Explicit multi-row safe guessing LP; private law redesign can trivialize state disclosure | Joint design when types affect payoffs or recommendation/privacy resources genuinely constrain the law. |
| Equilibrium selection | Strict terminal NE does not certify communication equilibrium | A decentralized protocol with explicit incentive protection for its randomization/role messages. |
| Symmetry | Anonymous identical histories cannot select distinct roles; private election randomness can be manipulated | Permissioned role tokens, verified observations or enforceable communication assumptions that change the boundary. |
| Learning | No convergence claim from CE feasibility or local DP | A specified repeated/local learning class reaching a useful outcome with strategic message deviations. |
| Robustness | Positive conditional margins retained in representation bounds; approximate-IC lower bound scoped separately | Uniform robustness under a stated deviation architecture, not default-policy prediction error. |
| Strategic interchangeability | Exact observer/coding results preserved; no new approximate synthesis theorem | A structural class with efficiently checkable all-deviation error and fixed memory budget. |
| Fairness/assignment | Independent impartial role assignment motivates a lottery; equal costs do not identify all strategic roles | Calibrated assignment preferences and strategic elicitation, rather than naming equal utilities fairness. |

The next intensive branch should compete on conceptual value, not merely add
one more parameter. The disclosure LP is explicitly an established-method
application, and the resource branch is not rewarded for theorem count.

Game Symmetry remains e896a5cae79c5c63a81be16f1577bc365e67a5a6. Neither changed;
no suspected canonical error arose. A separately motivated impartial lottery
over interchangeable TWO-role assignments gives selection probability1/2; in
P17-E2 it also minimizes the proved worst-case cost approximation among fixed
lotteries. This is a coincidence/consequence under the specified identical
cost domains, not a new fairness equivalence or an interpersonal utility axiom.
No-transfers type incentives force these lotteries to ignore the pair's costs;
local accurate opportunity information alone cannot be assumed truthfully
communicated. U1/U5/A1 calibration caveats therefore remain essential.

Read-once and repeated-worker formulas may have automorphisms, but the FPT
construction does not assume their efficient discovery or obtain communication
from splitting. The matching-face obstruction survives highly symmetric task
syntax: large symmetry does not imply a small simultaneous full-payoff LP.
It does not prohibit quotienting a symmetric objective or a small protected
projection, which can discard the hard coordinates.

## Publication recommendation and precise next gates

The old P14–16 collection should NOT be opened as a new manuscript simply by
combining short known-method consequences. Phase17 supplies a clearer possible
organizing question: EXACT representation of credible payoff regions and the
limits of syntactic task simplicity. A focused outline is now justified around
repeated-worker FPT and read-once/read-twice compression, with protected thinning
and restricted objective hardness as supporting tools. Exact source overlap
for that new representation statement must still be checked before a new
manuscript is created. No new title/repository/manuscript is authorized by
this internal assessment or was created.

Highest-value follow-ups: (i) fixed-small-projection or approximation limits,
where the matching face does NOT automatically answer the problem; (ii) a
strategic-only separation from an otherwise compact feasible-outcome polytope;
(iii) endogenous payoff-relevant joint disclosure/recommendation design;
(iv) repeated decentralized implementation with verified assumptions and
communication accounting. A result on any of these could supersede the current
representation theme. Do not optimize the old weighted-threshold constants.
