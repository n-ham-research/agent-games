# Strongest unresolved questions

1. For the finite private-cost volunteer family, what is the least verified or
incentive-compatible private communication that closes the public-local welfare
gap? The current externally informed mediator is not a truthful direct mechanism.
Specify message ownership, shared randomness, transfers and agent obedience
before stating bit or welfare guarantees. Compare with Bayesian information
design and communication-equilibrium literature first.
2. Under restricted observations of persistent state and actuator costs, when
can a cheap feedback controller track actual task targets? The full-state
formula is elementary. A new result must handle natural task equivalence,
partial information or constrained synchronization rather than rename splitting.
3. On bounded/nonnegative capability classes with decentralized row ownership,
which assignment decisions can be implemented from lossy observations? The
unrestricted rank theorem does not answer privacy, adaptive or bit complexity.
4. Which utility calibrations support an external task objective while cardinal
relabeling invariance is maintained? Do not infer welfare from role-specific
normalizations or treat software agents as moral patients by definition.
5. How do correlated type distributions or type-dependent permissions alter
interim automorphism groups and implementable recommendations? Preserve
ex-ante/interim/ex-post distinctions and positive-probability type assumptions.
6. What finite-time learning and communication guarantees survive for chosen
protocols? Gibbs stationarity, strict-improvement termination and no-regret
properties are different and do not automatically imply efficient selection.

Recommended next campaign: pursue question1 as the principal mathematical
thread; keep question2 as a contrasting operational model and question3 as an
information-theoretic support strand. Do not demand all three receive equal
paper space. No submission or external contact authorized.

1. Establish the precise originality of protected thinning and the constant-gap
   one-worker CE-payoff theorem against compact CE and public-good literature.
2. Find useful bridge-sensitive connectivity or task-circuit summaries whose
   size is bounded by a meaningful structural parameter; ordinary membership
   or graph treewidth alone is not enough to apply the proved summary lemma.
3. Optimize public disclosure subject to conditional obedience and positive
   margins, distinguishing signal composition and type-contingent plans.
4. Develop a genuinely substantial resource-synthesis theorem beyond finite
   adapter composition/nonnegative factorization/one-cut storage coding.
5. Keep learning/selection and independently calibrated opportunity fairness
   competitive; payoff-region feasibility alone establishes neither.

The prioritized questions are exact protected-thinning originality, controlled
repeated-worker deviation encodings, safe disclosure beyond one scalar tight row,
and resource-approximation synthesis beyond known exact observers. Keep calibrated
assignment, decentralized selection and learning competitive; no general fairness,
convergence or communication-free implementation follows from the LP results.
