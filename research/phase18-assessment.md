## The principal question: a precise partial positive answer

Unlike P17-X, G1 compares exactly the SAME action-marginal coordinates with
and without obedience, and colliding profiles remain physically possible in
both. Obedience alone introduces the independent-set geometry. This is an
incentive-caused exact representation separation in the stated projection.
However its equilibrium PAYOFF vector is constant; swapping the word payoff
for recommendation marginals would invalidate the conclusion. Best-shot G2
has positive incentives on its hard face but imposes the maximum work margin,
not a robust interval of ordinary CE constraints.

G3 gives the complementary standard lifting limitation: if the nonstrategic
moment polytope already compactly retains all output and action-conditioned
gain moments, adding2N obedience inequalities cannot cause an exponential
size increase. A compact coarse output polytope may discard hard correlations;
a small pure-payoff evaluator does not reconstruct those correlations.
This is not a new necessary-and-sufficient universal tractability theorem.

## Fixed projections: what was actually resolved

One worker payoff ALONE is always an interval, hence has a two-inequality
existential formulation. Its endpoints may still be NP-hard to compute, as
P16-H demonstrates. Efficient coefficient construction is different from
existence of a small LP. Reliability plus one payoff is already two-dimensional,
so the interval argument no longer settles that output contract.

P18-P supplies something constructive beyond that observation: for disjoint
protected matching edges in the K_(2m) coverage family, the reliability/
protected-payoff region has explicit O(k) inequalities and a polynomial exact
sampler-support construction, despite the full-region lower bound. Incident
roles and small residual graphs require separate arguments. No fixed-two-
coordinate superpolynomial EF family was proved; do not transfer the full
matching lower bound to it.

## Repeated-worker programme: retain, do not rename and repackage

P17-T1 remains valid. This phase has NOT found a substantive stronger overlap-
width class whose contribution exceeds standard constraint processing. The
general moment-lift condition explains why another supplied tree decomposition
corollary would not answer the originality request. Investigation was redirected
to the same-coordinate separation and exact projection rather than inventing
a width parameter. No stronger repeated-worker theorem is claimed.

## Recommendation: shift emphasis, keep a modest technical option

Do not create a dedicated manuscript yet. A modest representation paper could
be deliberately planned around G1/G2, P and the earlier repeated-worker result,
with each established-method dependency explicit. That would not yet meet the
ambition of a major new theory. A preliminary outline is saved separately,
not promoted to manuscript or publication approval.

Shift the next intensive exploration from formula-occurrence refinements to
(i) endogenous payoff-relevant information under partial self-awareness or
constrained sensors, and (ii) an ordinary-CE payoff-coordinate representation
separation without dominated work or a binding maximal margin. Keep fixed-small
projections as a competitive structural problem. The repeated fairness-memory
example should not consume another phase: close primary sources already cover
its central phenomenon. A genuine sequential implementation theorem must add
more than a public schedule of stage equilibria or a classic imitation proof.
