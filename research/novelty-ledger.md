## Phase 11

No new result is novelty-cleared. Screening/averaging are close to explicit
Battaglini–Palfrey2024 prior; binary verification is a nonobligatory inspection
special case; action-orbit systems are explicit Tewolde2025 prior. The local
factor representation and restricted welfare optimization are adaptations
requiring further overlap review. q-only FPT is not claimed as a separation
from the explicit normal-form baseline. AG-H1 is a direct standard-style
counting reduction. See phase11-literature.md and phase11-priorities.md.

## Phase 12

applications. V12-A adapts established verification inequalities. V12-C is the

but additional limited-punishment/verification overlap review is needed. No

## Phase 13

Important narrowing: Li2017 thesis §2.5.2 eq2.21 explicitly has allocated and
unallocated audit capacities and punishment-per-audit ordering. V12-C is a
candidate task-specific endogenous-loss/voluntary-implementation synthesis,
not a new audit-LP principle. Its independent final attack passes stated scope.

P13-P2's closed zero-CE-payoff criterion and P13-Q's player-transitive two-orbit
root construction are the strongest fresh candidates. General worst-CE-payoff
LPs, matching-pennies/counting-output reductions, CE thinning and one-dimensional
sampling are prior territory. Cavaliere2001 full text and exact q2 overlap remain
publication checks. P13-H/Qapprox/R are explicitly standard-method/direct
corollaries. No candidate is originality-cleared. The broad strategy permits
abandoning any branch when its structural increment is not significant.
