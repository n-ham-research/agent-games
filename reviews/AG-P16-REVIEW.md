## Actual primary-overlap narrowing

Weighted-voting relevance, general optimal-CE hardness, compact-CE separation
and approximate-CE quality barriers are established. The payoff gap is a short
restricted transfer, not a new NP-hard core. Read-once representations and
series-parallel connectivity translations are specifically prior Elkind et al.;
local-marginal exactness is prior junction-tree theory. The candidate increment
is a full costly-work CE-region sensitivity formulation, not network mapping.

Rassouli–Gündüz perfect-privacy rank-supported columns already imply disclosure
pair decomposition; transportation is a specialization. Sequential observer,
Schewe–Finkbeiner bounded synthesis and INDEX are direct primary comparisons,
so no new universal synthesis/equivalence theorem is claimed. The records do
not treat absence of a matching source as originality clearance.

## Retained counterexamples and limits

Repeated worker x AND x breaks the single-path sensitivity lift; it does not
prove that simplified task is hard. Tiny graph width does not control binary
capacities. Deterministic safe disclosure differs from randomized disclosure;
exact positive-margin safety is not tolerant obedience by definition. Observation
reconstruction must hold on all hidden-action/input paths; default behavior
is insufficient. A state decoder may require exponentially many modes but
only linearly many bits; external query/store resources must be charged.
