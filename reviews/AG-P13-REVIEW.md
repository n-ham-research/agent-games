## Strongest new candidates and independent attacks

* P13-P2: zero CE payoff forces pivotality on every success. Singleton agents
  then always abstain, imposing a success floor. Pair repairers constrain failed
  target work; large minimal coalitions and pair-only cases give exactly the
  stated alternatives. The attaining private law has only success, target-only
  failure and empty failure; every worker/abstainer deviation is checked. Root,
  computation and audit reviewers found no omitted repair state or proof defect.
* P13-P1: success-preserving deletion raises both unconditional CE obedience
  margins and all utilities. It can destroy punishment and private-report IC;
  both are shown by exact witnesses. Close persuasion/thinning literature is
  recorded. This is not an unrestricted mechanism-reduction theorem.
* P13-Q: the action0 slice is constant and action1 slice nonconstant, forbidding
  every action flip in the full exact automorphism group. Rotation forces the
  common-p invariant locus; the Lipschitz circuit term leaves a strictly
  decreasing advantage. The wrong-side regret bound extracts the counting
  threshold. Root/audit review found no defect. Full-group generators are not
  constructed, even though its actual action-orbit count is proved to be2.
* Qapprox: correct-sign confidence intervals preserve a root bracket without
  monotonicity. Fresh samples justify adaptive Hoeffding plus a union bound;
  dyadic sampling and explicit output costs are charged. This standard corollary
  prevents claiming ordinary-accuracy hardness from the fine-precision theorem.
* P13-H/W: pointed histogram success/workload weights and the fallback/gate
  counting extraction were independently reconstructed. Exact optimization is
  within the declared mechanism architecture; its value lower bound does not
  establish mechanism-output hardness.
* Root robustness: positive conditional work-obedience margin implies the
  displayed payoff floor. The audit reviewer checked the inequality and zero-
  probability recommendation convention. A sharp complete-information example
  is tested, with no stronger bounded-rationality interpretation asserted.

## Scope and source uncertainty

P13-P2 is complete-information private CE, not an automatic post-audit mechanism
when others' costs remain private. Its zero face relies on weak obedience;
robustness is separately bounded. Closed-form zero-payoff feasibility assumes
minimal-coalition information is available and is not a polynomial membership-
oracle discovery algorithm. Cavaliere2001 public-good correlation full text is
still uninspected; both candidate novelty claims remain provisional.
